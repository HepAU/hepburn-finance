New Afterpay purchase

Punch in the details from the Afterpay confirmation screen — total, first instalment date, where you bought from. Each instalment becomes its own dated bill so the calendar shows them distinctly.

What happens: Four (or N) one-off bills get created at fortnightly intervals starting from your first instalment date, each named "Afterpay · [store] (1 of 4)" through "(4 of 4)". Each one shows up individually on the calendar and falls off after it's been paid. Edit or delete any of them later from the bill list.
